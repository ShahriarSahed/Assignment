Contact and service request

Use this form to tell us about your vehicle and the service you need.

We will call you to confirm the appointment time. Your preferred day is a request, not a confirmed booking.

Online service request

Fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. Your draft is saved only in this browser on this device.

Enter the name we should use when contacting you.

For example, ABC-123. Do not enter a phone number here.

Describe the problem or service you need in at least 10 characters.

We will call this number to confirm the appointment. Use digits, spaces, +, brackets or hyphens.

Add an email address only if you would also like us to have it.

Choose today or a future date. We will call to confirm a time.

About saving and sending this form

This form checks your information in the browser and keeps a draft on this device. It does not send an email or store a request at the workshop. Do not save personal details on a shared device; use “Clear saved draft” when you are finished.

The workshop's IT partner must connect the form to the workshop's email before real online requests can be received.

Appointment confirmation

After receiving a service request, the workshop will call the customer to confirm the appointment time. Sending a request does not confirm a booking.
