# Website plan – Autokorjaamo Hakola

## 1. Goal and must-haves

The goal is to create a simple website for Autokorjaamo Hakola, a small car repair shop in Kauhava run by Pertti Hakola and his son.

The website must:

- Explain what the workshop does.
- Present its servicing, brake, tyre, inspection preparation and general repair services.
- Mention that the workshop also works with vans and farm vehicles.
- Let visitors find a service request form easily.
- Ask for the vehicle registration number, a description of the problem, contact details and a preferred day.
- Help prevent a phone number being entered in the vehicle registration field.
- Prevent the visitor from choosing a date in the past.
- Explain that the workshop calls the customer to confirm the appointment time.
- Use four connected pages with the same navigation menu.

The first version checks the form in the browser but does not send it. The workshop's IT partner must connect the form to the workshop's email later.

## 2. Visitors

1. **Car owners** who need servicing, brake work, tyre services or general repairs.
2. **Van owners** who need repair or maintenance work.
3. **Farm vehicle owners** who need repair services for their vehicles.

These visitor groups are based on the vehicle types and services mentioned in the customer brief.

## 3. Content plan

| Page | Purpose | Main content | Visitor's next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | Introduce the workshop | Business name, location, short introduction and service summary | Open Services or Contact |
| `about.html` | Explain who the workshop is | Pertti Hakola, his son, location and types of vehicles | Open Services |
| `listing.html` | Explain available work | Servicing, brakes, tyres, inspection preparation and general repairs; vans and farm vehicles | Open the service request form |
| `contact.html` | Collect service request details | Registration number, problem, name, phone, optional email and preferred day | Submit the form for checking; wait for a call to confirm the time |

## 5. Tone and key messages

The tone should be friendly, clear and practical.

Key messages:

- Autokorjaamo Hakola is a small, family-run repair shop in Kauhava.
- The workshop repairs cars, vans and farm vehicles.
- Customers can describe their service needs using an online request form.
